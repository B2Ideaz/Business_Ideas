New Funding Product for Startups ⌄

A desktop platform that helps founders discover and organize funding opportunities from one searchable workspace.

About Genvium

Genvium helps startups discover, organize, and track grants, tax credits, accelerators, startup competitions, and investor opportunities from a centralized searchable platform designed to reduce friction and help founders move faster.

How Does It Work?

Genvium is currently available as a native desktop application for Apple Silicon Macs. Users simply download the application to access a searchable collection of startup funding opportunities and supporting resources.

Instead of searching dozens of websites individually, founders can use one platform to research, organize, and keep track of relevant opportunities.

Key Features

  • Centralized funding search
  • Grant discovery
  • Tax credit database
  • Accelerator and incubator listings
  • Startup competition discovery
  • Investor opportunity listings
  • Search and filtering tools
  • Saved opportunities
  • Application tracking
  • Genvium Plus AI assistant

What Makes It Interesting?

Funding opportunities for startups are often scattered across government websites, nonprofit organizations, accelerators, universities, corporate programs, and investor platforms.

Genvium is working to make these fragmented funding resources easier to discover by bringing them together into one searchable experience. Rather than replacing the original sources, the platform aims to simplify how founders find, organize, and revisit opportunities throughout their funding journey.

Availability

  • Desktop application
  • Apple Silicon Macs
  • Developed by Auduvant Technologies
